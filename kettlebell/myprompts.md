- Core 4 is a full-body kettlebell workout using one bell, done as repeated rounds of 10 reps per move.
- Move 1: Thrusters, 10 reps. A full-body push hitting quads, shoulders, triceps and upper chest, with the core engaged; hardest move, so done first.
- Move 2: Rows, 10 reps (five per side). Works back, biceps and forearms; pull-ups are an alternative.
- Move 3: Push-ups, 10 reps. Standard push-ups here, though kettlebell push-ups are a favorite for chest strength.
- Move 4: Swings, 10 reps. A ballistic posterior-chain finisher; deadlifts or snatches can be substituted.
- Programming: three 30-minute sessions per week, roughly five to eight rounds, here with a 24 kg bell.
- It covers upper and lower push and pull patterns, builds muscle and doubles as cardio.
